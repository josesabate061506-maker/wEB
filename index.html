WHO WE ARE

About PediGo

PediGo is a proposed digital transportation platform that aims to improve how passengers connect with local pedicab drivers. By combining local transportation with digital booking, PediGo seeks to provide a more organized and convenient ride experience.

✓

Our Mission

To make local pedicab transportation more accessible, convenient, and organized through digital innovation.

✓

Our Vision

To become a trusted local digital transportation solution that supports passengers and pedicab drivers.

✓

Our Objectives

To simplify ride booking, support local drivers, improve access to transportation information, and promote community-based mobility.
